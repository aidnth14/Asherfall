================================================================================

1. ITEM SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Items in Asherfall populate the world as harvestable node drops, loot pickups,
and player inventory resources. All item sprites reside in client/assets/items/.

Key Item Capabilities:
  - 3D Physics Simulation: When dropped from trees or boulders, items launch with
    upward velocity (vz = 160-260) and random horizontal vectors (vx, vy = +/-1.8).
  - Gravity & Elastic Bouncing: Downward gravitational acceleration (-800 px/s^2),
    with restitution bounce (-0.4) on ground contact until coming to a rest.
  - Ground Shadow Casting: Every resting or airborne item casts an isometric
    ellipse shadow onto the tile surface below.
  - Proximity Magnetization: Items within 2.5 tiles smoothly accelerate toward
    the player.
  - Instant Collection: Items within 0.85 tiles are picked up, playing pickup.wav
    and triggering a floating green "+1 [Item]" text popup.

3. INVENTORY HUD (draw_inventory_hud)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Position: Top-left, below weapon HUD (x=16, y=96).
- Design: Glassmorphic panel with subtle borders (RGB 50, 65, 85, alpha 230).
- Contents: Real-time counts and 18x18 mini-icons for all gathered resources:
    * Log (Wood Loam)
    * Wood (Planks)
    * Stick (Twigs)
    * Coal (Carbon)
    * Iron (Ferrum)
    * Copper (Bronze)
    * Gold (Aurum)
    * Diamond (Gem)


4. ADVENTURER'S BACKPACK & HOTBAR SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 5-Cell Wooden Hotbar Dock:
    * Centered at bottom screen with dedicated slot keybadges (1-5).
    * Left-clicking or pressing 1-5 immediately equips any weapon or tool in that slot.
    * Active slot is highlighted with a gold border and ambient glow.
    * Flanked by "BAG [E]" button (left) and "CRAFT [C]" button (right).

- 16-Slot Adventurer's Backpack:
    * Opened/closed anytime via [E], Esc, or clicking "BAG [E]".
    * Spawns with complete starter survival loadout:
        - Hotbar: Sword x1, Pickaxe x1, Axe x1, Apple x5 (+25 HP), Wood x20
        - Backpack: AK47 x1, Ammo x60, Bread x4 (+35 HP), Meat x2 (+45 HP), Brick x10, Key x1, Book x1
    * Interactive Stack Management & Controls:
        - [Left-Click]: Pick up, place, or swap item stacks.
        - [Right-Click]: Split stack in half / place 1 item into slot.
        - [Shift + Left-Click]: Instant quick-transfer between Backpack and Hotbar.
        - [Shift + Right-Click] or [F] / [Space]: Direct use/eat/equip hovered slot item.
        - [Q]: Drop 1 item into world with parabolic arc physics (Shift+[Q] drops full stack).
        - [1-5]: While hovering any slot, instantly swaps it with that hotbar slot.
        - Click outside backpack area: Drops held cursor item directly into the world.
