================================================================================
ASHERFALL - CORE MECHANICS & GAME FEEL ("JUICE") SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. CELESTIAL ORBITAL RING COMBAT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Earth-Sun Astronomical Analog:
  - The player's avatar acts as the central Sun.
  - Held weapons, swords, and tools orbit on an elliptical ring (the Earth).
  - Normal State: The weapon rests on the perimeter, dynamically tracking the
    cursor aim angle without ever straying outside the ring boundary.

B. 360-Degree Celestial Revolution:
  - Left-clicking with a sword or tool triggers a full 360-degree orbital sweep
    around the player in 0.20 seconds.
  - Formula: tool_ang = base_angle + (progress * 2.0 * pi)

C. Simultaneous Self-Axis Rotation:
  - While completing the orbital revolution, the weapon spins rapidly on its own
    axis/line (2 full 360-degree spins, 720 degrees total):
    self_spin_angle = progress * 4.0 * pi
  - Composite Visual Angle: rot_angle = -(tool_ang) + self_spin_angle

D. Luminous Slash Trail Ribbons:
  - A 16-segment fading energy ribbon traces behind the blade tip along the ring.
  - Electric cyan-white for swords; fiery gold for axes; steel-blue for pickaxes.


2. GAME FEEL ("JUICE") & PHYSICS SIMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Visceral Hit-Stop (Micro-Pause Impact Crunch):
  - On connecting with an enemy or mining node, a 35ms micro-pause is triggered
    (S.hitstop_timer = 0.035). Delta time (dt) is scaled down by 85% for one or
    two frames, imparting immense physical weight and tactile punch.

B. Enemy Knockback & Momentum Physics:
  - Striking an enemy imparts a physical launch velocity vector (e.knock_vx, e.knock_vy):
      * Sword Spin: 12.0 impulse
      * Hammer Cleave: 14.0 impulse
      * Bullets: 7.5 impulse
  - Ground Drag: Velocity decays via exponential damping: math.exp(-8.5 * dt).
  - Elastic Obstacle Bounce: Collisions with solid rocks/cliffs rebound with
    restitution coefficient -0.35.

C. Bouncy Damage Popups with Gravity:
  - Damage numbers spawn with initial upward pop (vy = -55.0) and random horizontal
    drift (vx = +/-20.0).
  - Downward gravity (+130.0 px/s^2) curves the numbers downward in a natural arc.
  - Fresh hits pop with a dynamic +30% scale before settling and fading.

D. Directional Slash Shockwaves & Starburst Sparks:
  - On sword impacts, directional slash line particles (is_slash = True) cut
    through the air aligned with the attack angle.
  - 12 starburst sparks spray outward at speeds up to 200 px/s.

E. Dynamic Camera Recoil Shake:
  - Screen shake is completely REMOVED for tools and swords.
  - Screen shake for firearms is significantly reduced (subtle recoil: 0.15 for pistols, 1.1 for shotgun, 1.2 for sniper) with gentle, non-jarring camera feedback.


3. INTERACTIVE MINING & WOODCHOPPING ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Unified Hit Resolution (execute_mining_or_chopping):
  - Automatically targets cursor hover tile (up to 4.2 tiles reach) and forward
    aim cone projections (1.0, 1.8, 2.5, 3.2 tiles ahead).
  - Woodchopping: Targets trees, logs, and stumps (IDs 48-60). STRICTLY REQUIRES AXE.
    Axe deals 35-52 dmg, plays chop.wav, emits sawdust particles, rattles trunk.
    Striking with any other tool/weapon deflects with a "Requires Axe" notification.
  - Mining: Targets boulders (IDs 62, 64, 65, 67, 68) and ore bedrock (IDs 100, 101). STRICTLY REQUIRES PICKAXE.
    Pickaxe deals 35-52 dmg, plays mine.wav, stone sparks, rattles the rock.
    Striking with any other tool/weapon deflects with a "Requires Pickaxe" notification.
  - Bullets & Firearms: Obstacle collisions deflect harmlessly without damaging logs or stone nodes.
  - Node Depletion: At 0 HP, plays break.wav, bursts 14 stone/wood particles,
    clears the prop, and spawns 2-4 physical bouncing item drops.


4. PHYSICAL DROPS & PROXIMITY MAGNETIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Parabolic Bounce Physics:
  - Dropped items launch with upward velocity (vz = 160-260) and scatter horizontally.
  - Gravity (-800 px/s^2) pulls items down; ground collisions trigger an elastic
    bounce (vz *= -0.4) with ground shadow projection.

B. Proximity Magnetization:
  - When the player walks within 2.5 tiles, items are attracted toward the player
    with velocity proportional to proximity.

C. Instant Inventory Collection:
  - When within 0.85 tiles, the item is collected into S.inventory.
  - Plays chime audio (pickup.wav) and spawns a floating green "+1 [Item]" popup.


5. USER INTERFACE & NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Real-Time Glass Inventory HUD (draw_inventory_hud):
  - Displays gathered resource counts (Logs, Wood, Sticks, Coal, Iron, Copper,
    Gold, Diamonds) with 18x18 mini icons in a sleek glass panel.

B. Precision Compass System (draw_compass):
  - Fixed dial at bottom-right of the screen.
  - Features stationary N, S, E, W compass dial face.
  - Red needle cover rotates dynamically to point directly toward Base Camp (0, 0).

C. Heart Health Bar (draw_hearts):
  - Renders red hearts showing current player vitality; vibrates on damage taken.

D. Keybinds & Controls:
  - [W, A, S, D] / Arrows: Screen-relative isometric movement
  - [Space]: Jump (In-world)
  - [Left Mouse]: Attack / Shoot / 360-degree Celestial Tool Spin / Mine
  - [Right Mouse]: Secondary action / Mine / Chop / Use consumable from active hotbar
  - [1 - 5]: Select Hotbar Slot (1-5) and automatically equip stored weapon/tool
  - [Shift + Mouse Wheel]: Cycle arsenal (weapons and tools)
  - [E]: Toggle 16-slot Adventurer's Backpack Inventory
  - [F]: Interact / Open Chests / Loot Breakable Containers / Use hovered item
  - [C]: Toggle Survival Crafting Bench
  - [Q]: Drop hovered item or cursor item to world (Shift+Q drops full stack)
  - [Tab]: Toggle visual theme (Dirt vs Stone)
  - [Esc]: Pause Menu / Settings / Close Backpack
  - [T] / [Enter]: Open In-Game Chat
  - [V] (Hold): Push-to-Talk Voice Chat

E. Xbox Wireless Controller / Gamepad Input (Full Dual-Stick Integration):
  - Left Analog Stick (Axes 0, 1): Full 360-degree screen-relative isometric movement.
    Pushing stick UP moves visually UP on screen; deadzone 0.18 with linear ramp.
  - Right Analog Stick (Axes 2, 3): 360-degree twin-stick cursor aiming angle.
  - [RT] / [RB] / [X Button]: Primary attack, firearm trigger, mining/chopping strike,
    and 360-degree celestial tool spin.
  - [LT] / [B Button] / [L3 Click]: Combat roll / sprint dash.
  - [A Button]: Jump / interact.
  - [Y Button] / [LB]: Cycle weapon / tool loadout in hotbar.
  - [D-Pad]: Quick-slot navigation (Left/Right cycle hotbar, Up/Down theme toggle).
  - Hotplugging: Dynamic connect/disconnect detection without restarting game.



6. MULTIPLAYER NETWORKING & AUDIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Relay Server Architecture (server/server.py):
  - High-performance asynchronous non-blocking TCP socket server.
  - Broadcasts position, animation, aim angle, and state packets at 60Hz.

B. Spatial Voice Chat (client/voice.py):
  - Low-latency UDP voice streaming using Opus audio compression and PyAudio.
  - Proximity volume attenuation and floating microphone indicators.


7. FRAME RATE REGULATION & HARDWARE OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Responsive 60 FPS Target:
  - Active Gameplay & Menus: Butter-smooth 60.0 FPS target (16.6ms frame pacing)
    eliminates input latency and ensures fluid character motion and combat.
  - Minimized / Inactive: Throttles to 20 FPS to conserve energy when the window
    loses focus or is minimized.

B. Non-Busy Hybrid Sleep Governor (client/main.py):
  - Eliminates 100% busy-wait spin loops by calculating remaining frame margin and
    yielding thread execution to the OS kernel via non-busy sleep (time.sleep).
  - Aligns frame delivery with pygame.time.Clock for rock-solid 60 FPS pacing without
    sacrificing responsiveness or jittering physics steps.

C. Isometric Sliding Window Buffer (client/iso.py):
  - Pre-renders a padded viewport canvas (PAD=96px, ~3-6 tiles on each edge).
  - Directly blits the cached surface with offset when the camera drifts within the margin.
  - Avoids re-blitting 1,000+ individual isometric tiles every frame, dropping tile
    rendering overhead by over 95%.
  - Version-based cache invalidation (world.version) prevents re-rendering on tile queries.

D. Audio Thread & Surface Compositing Optimization:
  - SDL Mixer pre-initialized with 8192 audio buffer, dropping CoreAudio background
    thread CPU usage from 2.5% down to ~0.9%.
  - Pre-composed full-screen menu overlay (S._menu_dim_vignette) eliminates redundant
    full-screen per-pixel alpha blending copies.

8. SWIMMING DYNAMICS & PLAYER DEATH LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Freshwater Swimming Dynamics:
  - Detection: Client checks Iso.is_water(gx, gy) across river channels, ponds, and rapids.
  - Speed Damping: Moving through water tiles while grounded (z <= JUMP_CLEAR) reduces
    movement speed to 65% of land speed, giving authentic aquatic resistance.
  - Concentric Water Ripples: Two expanding elliptical wake rings pulse outward at the waterline.
  - Buoyancy Wave Bobbing: Vertical harmonic oscillation (sin 5.2 Hz) bobs avatar body in rhythm.
  - Waterline Foam Wake: Submerges lower 35% of player avatar beneath water level with a
    glowing cyan-white foam wake ring.

B. Player Death & Respawn Lifecycle:
  - Trigger: Taking fatal damage (HP <= 0) initiates player death sequence.
  - State Locking: Disables movement inputs, weapons, and tool interactions.
  - Collapsing Spin Animation: Avatar shrinks and rotates down into the terrain over 2.0 seconds.
  - Ascending Ghost Soul Orb: Radiant ethereal cyan spirit orb rises 65px above death point
    accompanied by orbiting spirit wisps.
  - Respawn Banner: Displays "RESPAWNING IN Xs..." countdown overlay above the death location.
  - Base Camp Respawn: Resets player to 100 HP, repositions avatar at Base Camp spawn cell,
    snaps camera back smoothly, and displays a glowing green "REVIVED!" celebration popup.
