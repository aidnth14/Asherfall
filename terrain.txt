ASHERFALL - TERRAIN & WORLD GENERATION SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asherfall uses an infinite, deterministic isometric terrain generation system
implemented in client/iso.py. The map is completely procedural and boundless in
all directions; no static grid is saved to disk. Every tile at world coordinates
(gx, gy) is derived on-the-fly via mathematical hash functions and multi-octave
fractal value noise.

Key Constants:
  - Source Tile Resolution: 32x32 pixels (TILE_SRC = 32)
  - Screen Projections: Isometric 2:1 ratio (HALF_W = 16 * scale, HALF_H = 8 * scale)
  - Zoom Scale Range: 1x to 3x (MIN_SCALE = 1, MAX_SCALE = 3)
  - Noise Feature Scale: 30.0 (controls frequency of biomes, patches, and hills)
  - Origin Sanctuary: Radius of 4.5 to 6.0 tiles around (0, 0) is protected from
    river/pond cutting to ensure a clean base camp.


2. THEMES & BIOMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The terrain engine supports live switchable themes (toggleable via Tab key):

A. DIRT THEME ("dirt")
  - Ground Tiles: Cubes 0-18, and 21 (rich earthen soil, grass patches, mossy dirt)
  - Surface Props: IDs 48-60 (pine trees, oak trunks, stumps, deadfall logs, mounds)
  - Prop Density: 88% spawn rate (prop_rate = 0.88)
  - Base Clear Color: RGB(54, 38, 30) - dark rich loam

B. STONE THEME ("stone")
  - Ground Tiles: IDs 61, 63, 66, 69 (granite slabs, flagstones, crushed gravel)
  - Surface Props: IDs 62, 64, 65, 67, 68 (granite boulders, stone monoliths, rock spires)
  - Prop Density: 86% spawn rate (prop_rate = 0.86)
  - Base Clear Color: RGB(40, 44, 52) - cold mountain slate


3. PROCEDURAL NOISE & ELEVATION ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Elevation and landmass sculpting rely on deterministic pseudo-random hash functions
and value noise interpolation:

A. Hash Function (_hash01)
  - Prime multiplier hash: (x * 374761393 + y * 668265263 + seed * 2246822519)
  - Bit-shifts and non-linear mixing for zero spatial correlation.
  - Normalizes output to [0.0, 1.0].

B. Hermite Smoothstep Curve (_smooth)
  - Formula: t * t * (3.0 - 2.0 * t)
  - Guarantees continuous C1 first-derivative gradients across grid cell boundaries.

C. Multi-Octave Fractal Value Noise (fractal)
  - 4 Octaves with lacunarity of 2.0 and persistence of 0.5.
  - Seed-offset sampling prevents octave alignment artifacts.
  - Produces natural rolling hills, plateau cliffs, and sunken valleys.

D. Elevation Calculation (elev)
  - World coordinates (gx, gy) map to visual screen offset sy -= elev(gx, gy).
  - Normal terrain ranges from 0 to 48 height pixels.
  - Mountain peaks reach up to 64+ height pixels.
  - Rivers and ponds depress elevation by -8 to -10 pixels, producing natural
    sunken embankments along shorelines.


4. HYDROLOGY & WATER AUTOTILING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Water is categorized into deep bodies, shallow shores, freshwater rivers, and rapids:

A. River Generation (_is_river)
  - Domain Warping: Perlin coordinate displacement wx, wy (amplitude 8.0).
  - Function: riv_val = fractal((gx + wx) / 55.0, (gy + wy) / 55.0, seed + 501)
  - Centerline Distance: riv_d = abs(riv_val - 0.5) * 2.0
  - Dynamic Meander Width: riv_w = 0.038 + 0.008 * sin(gx * 0.12 + gy * 0.15)
  - Condition: riv_d < riv_w (creates winding ribbons across the landscape).

B. Pond & Lake Generation (_is_pond)
  - Spatial Partitioning: 28x28 grid cells (cell_size = 28).
  - Hashed Cell Centers: Offset by +/- 7 tiles inside each chunk.
  - Radius: 2.2 to 3.8 tiles with radial noise distortion.

C. Complete Water Tile Catalog:
  - Deep Water (Dark Blue): IDs 86, 87, 88, 89 (used in deep basins)
  - Shallow Shore Tiles: IDs 96, 97, 98, 99
  - Freshwater Open Water: ID 104 (calm crystal blue water, no lime highlight)
  - Directional River Banks (with precise lime highlights):
      * 105: Bank with lime highlight on North-East (top-right)
      * 106: Bank with lime highlight on North-West (top-left)
      * 107: Bank with lime highlight on South-East (bottom-right)
      * 108: Bank with lime highlight on South-West (bottom-left)
      * 109: North outer corner (lime on NW + NE)
      * 110: South outer corner (lime on SW + SE)
      * 111: West outer corner (lime on NW + SW)
      * 112: East outer corner (lime on NE + SE)
      * 113: Deep inlet / concave curve (lime on 3 sides)
      * 114: Whitewater rapids and frothing waves (spawned in high-velocity bends)
  - Stepping Stones & Water Rocks: IDs 72-81 (natural granite boulders resting in blue water)

D. Autotiling Algorithm (autotile_water_cell)
  - Inspects 4-neighborhood (N: (gx, gy-1), S: (gx, gy+1), W: (gx-1, gy), E: (gx+1, gy)).
  - Matches water vs land adjacency to choose exact bank and corner indices.
  - Ensures seamless visual orientation of lime waterline reflections.


5. ORE VEINS & GEOLOGICAL DEPOSITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Embedded within stone terrain and mountainous formations:
  - Tile ID 100: Iron, Coal & Copper Ore Bedrock (grey rocky slab with dark mineral seams).
  - Tile ID 101: Gold & Diamond Ore Bedrock (crystalline sparkle and golden ore flecks).
  - Hardness: 100 HP. Yields precious bouncy ore drops upon pickaxe depletion.


6. SOLIDITY & OBSTACLE COLLISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The function iso.solid(gx, gy) enforces navigation physics:
  - All tree props (48-60) and boulder props (62, 64, 65, 67, 68) block movement.
  - Stepping stone props (72-81) and ore bedrock tiles (100, 101) block direct traversal.
  - Deep water bodies are non-walkable unless bridged by stepping stones.
  - Players and monsters slide smoothly along solid diagonal tile edges.
