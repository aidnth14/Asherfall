1. TOOLS ROSTER & AFFINITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tools serve dual functions: harvesting environmental resources (woodchopping and
mining) and close-quarters melee combat.

A. SWORD ("Blade of Asher")
  - Damage: 45 base (boosted to 58 on celestial spin)
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.85 tiles (Extra wide sweeping arc)
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.35 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Enemies: Cleaves in a full 360-degree radius on celestial spin
      * Trees / Logs: 20-35 damage per strike
      * Rocks: 12 damage
  - Special FX: Electric cyan-white celestial trail ribbon and directional cuts.

B. AXE ("Lumberjack Cleaver")
  - Damage: 25 base (boosted to 35-52 vs trees and logs)
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.45 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Trees / Stumps / Deadfall (IDs 48-60): High efficiency; chops down trees
        in 2-3 strikes, yielding bouncing logs, sticks, and wood planks.
      * Enemies: 28 damage with outward knockback.
  - Special FX: Fiery amber-gold sweep arc, flying sawdust and wood chips.

C. PICKAXE ("Miner's Mattock")
  - Damage: 25 base (boosted to 35-52 vs rocks and ore veins)
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.45 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Boulders / Spires (IDs 62, 64, 65, 67, 68): Breaks boulders into stone and ores.
      * Ore Bedrock (IDs 100, 101): Shatters deep deposits to extract iron, coal,
        copper, gold, and diamonds.
      * Enemies: 28 damage with armor-cracking impact.
  - Special FX: High metallic ringing ping (mine.wav), bright white stone sparks.

D. HAMMER ("War Maul")
  - Damage: 24 base (boosted to 35 vs rocky armor)
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.45 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Knockback Impulse: 14.0 (Highest knockback in the game)
  - Target Affinity:
      * Shatters stone props and knocks large enemies across terrain.
  - Special FX: Low sub-bass impact thud, expanding ground shockwave.

E. SHOVEL ("Excavator Spade")
  - Damage: 18 base (boosted to 28 vs dirt/gravel)
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.20 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Soil scraping and light rock chipping.

F. MALLET ("Woodsman's Mallet")
  - Damage: 22 base
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.20 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Splitting logs and medium blunt impacts.

G. SCYTHE / HOE ("Harvester's Reaping Blade")
  - Damage: 20 base
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.20 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Clearing high brush, reeds, and crops.

H. FISHING ROD ("Angler's Line")
  - Damage: 15 base
  - Hitbox Reach: 2.20 tiles
  - Attack Rate / Cooldown: 0.40 seconds
  - Target Affinity:
      * Water interaction and recreational fishing.


2. CELESTIAL ORBITAL RING SYSTEM (EARTH-SUN DYNAMICS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When holding any melee weapon or tool, the tool does not sit statically in the
player's hand. Instead, it orbits the player on a mathematically defined ellipse:
  - Conceptual Analog: Player is the Sun (origin); Tools are Earth in orbit.
  - Ring Dimensions: rx = 28 * scale, ry = 18 * scale (expands to 32x20 during spins).
  - Normal State: The tool rests on the orbital ring perimeter, pointing toward
    the mouse cursor, and cannot move beyond the perimeter boundary.

A. The 360-Degree Celestial Revolution:
  - Trigger: Left-Click (tap or hold) when holding any tool or sword.
  - Revolution: The weapon executes a complete 360-degree circular orbit around
    the player in 0.20 seconds (ultra-fast, snappy feel).
  - Formula: tool_ang = base_aim_angle + (progress * 2 * pi)

B. Self-Spin On Weapon's Own Axis:
  - Mirroring Earth's diurnal rotation, the weapon simultaneously spins rapidly
    around its own central point/line during the orbital transit.
  - Self-Spin: Completes two full 360-degree rotations (720 degrees total):
    self_spin_angle = progress * 4 * pi
  - Composite Visual Angle: rot_angle = -(tool_ang) + self_spin_angle

C. Luminous Slash Trail Ribbons:
  - Sweeping behind the weapon during the spin is a multi-layered luminous arc
    sampled across 16 geometric segments along the ring perimeter.
  - Segment alpha tapers off from 240 down to 0, producing a brilliant comet tail.

D. Hitbox Cleave:
  - During the 360-degree spin, all enemies within reach (2.85 tiles for Sword,
    2.45 tiles for Tools) are hit, taking full damage and receiving a radial
    outward launch impulse away from the player.


3. VISCERAL HIT-STOP & TACTILE JUICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Hit-Stop (Micro-Pause Impact):
  - On connecting with an enemy, a 35ms micro-pause (S.hitstop_timer = 0.035)
    slows game physics by 85%, providing visceral crunch and impact weight.

B. Screen Shake & Directional Flash:
  - Screen shake is strictly REMOVED (0.0) for tools and swords, ensuring clean, fluid vision during melee combat and mining.
  - Directional slash line particles burst along the angle of impact.

C. Quick Hotkeys:
  - Key [X]: Instantly equips Axe.
  - Key [C]: Instantly equips Pickaxe.
  - Key [E]: Cycles next weapon/tool in the hotbar.
