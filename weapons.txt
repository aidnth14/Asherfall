1. BALLISTICS ENGINE OVERVIEW (client/weapons.py & client/game.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Firearms in Asherfall operate via real-time physical projectile simulation in 3D
isometric world space.

Key Ballistics Features:
  - Exact Muzzle Elevation: Bullets spawn at the true visual tip of the gun barrel:
      barrel_px = 16 * scale * 1.07
      gun_z = elev(player_x, player_y) + player_z + player_radius
  - Mathematical Projection: Screen aim angles are mapped into isometric velocity
    vectors (vwx, vwy) using inverse isometric coordinate transforms:
      vwx = ((svx / HALF_W) + (svy / HALF_H)) / 2.0
      vwy = ((svy / HALF_H) - (sox / HALF_W)) / 2.0
  - Visual Bullet Rotation: Bullet sprites are rotated on-screen by their screen
    aim angle so projectile tips always face the direction of flight.
  - Obstacle & Prop Collision: Bullets collide with solid terrain, cliffs, trees,
    and rocks, playing collision audio (play_bullet_collision_sfx) and ejecting
    earthen or metallic ricochet sparks.
  - Enemy Impact: Imparts immediate damage, physical knockback momentum vector
    (e.knock_vx, e.knock_vy = kdx * 7.5, kdy * 7.5), and triggers screen shake.

3. HUD & AMMO DISPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Active Weapon Card (draw_weapon_hud):
  - Glassmorphic card positioned at top-left (x=16, y=16).
  - Shows high-resolution icon of the equipped firearm or tool.
  - Displays Weapon Name, Fire Mode badge ("SOLO", "BURST", "AUTO", "SPREAD", "MELEE"),
    and damage rating in gold lettering.
