ASHERFALL - ORES, MINERALS & MINING SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. MINERAL & ORE CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ores in Asherfall are raw mineral nodes embedded throughout the procedural terrain.
They can be extracted using Pickaxes, Hammers, and explosive weaponry.

A. COAL ("Anthracite Ore")
  - Asset: items/ore/coal.png
  - Color Theme: Slate Charcoal (RGB 160, 165, 175)
  - Abundance: High (Common)
  - Occurrence:
      * Granite boulders and surface rock props (IDs 62, 64, 65, 67, 68)
      * Bedrock mineral seams (Tile ID 100)
  - Uses: Smelting fuel, torch crafting, and industrial energy.

B. COPPER ("Chalcopyrite Ore")
  - Asset: items/ore/copper_ore.png
  - Color Theme: Warm Bronze / Copper (RGB 225, 150, 95)
  - Abundance: Medium-High (Abundant)
  - Occurrence:
      * Mountain rock spires and stepping stone outcroppings
      * Bedrock mineral seams (Tile ID 100)
  - Uses: Electrical wiring, basic tools, and alloy metallurgy.

C. IRON ("Hematite Ore")
  - Asset: items/ore/iron_ore.png
  - Color Theme: Steel Silver (RGB 210, 215, 225)
  - Abundance: Medium
  - Occurrence:
      * Dense granite boulders and cliff outcroppings
      * Bedrock mineral seams (Tile ID 100 & 101)
  - Uses: High-grade armor, firearm frames, and structural components.

D. GOLD ("Native Aurum Ore")
  - Asset: items/ore/gold_ore.png
  - Color Theme: Radiant Gold (RGB 255, 220, 60)
  - Abundance: Rare
  - Occurrence:
      * Deep mountain peaks and mineral seams (Tile ID 101)
      * Deep bedrock clusters
  - Uses: Advanced optics, electronic relays, and trade currency.

E. DIAMOND ("Crystalline Gemstone")
  - Asset: items/ore/diamond.png
  - Color Theme: Brilliant Cyan (RGB 110, 230, 255)
  - Abundance: Very Rare (Legendary)
  - Occurrence:
      * Exclusive to rich mineral seams (Tile ID 101) in high elevation ranges
  - Uses: Tier-3 tool blades, armor reinforcement, and high-energy focal lenses.


2. MINING ENGINE & DEPLETION DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Node Health Tracking:
  - Every mineable boulder prop or ore bedrock tile has 100 HP, tracked in
    S.prop_health[(cx, cy)].

B. Tool Damage Affinity:
  - Pickaxe: Deals 35-52 damage per hit (Destroys node in 2 to 3 hits).
  - Hammer / Shovel: Deals 22-37 damage per hit (Destroys node in 3 to 5 hits).
  - Sword / Axes: Deals 12-15 damage per hit (Inefficient but possible).
  - Unarmed / Non-tools: 5 damage per hit.

C. Visual & Audio Feedback per Strike:
  - Resonant metallic ping audio: play_sfx(S, "mine", 0.55).
  - Stone sparks and grey mineral dust particles burst from impact point.
  - Floating damage numbers: -35 or -52 hover upward with bouncy gravity physics.
  - Prop Shaking: Target node visually rattles (prop_shake) on impact.

D. Node Depletion (Shattering):
  - When HP drops to <= 0:
      * Shatter Audio: play_sfx(S, "break", 0.70).
      * Heavy Debris: 14 stone and spark particles explode outward.
      * World Transformation: The rock prop is cleared, or the ore ground tile
        (100/101) transitions to plain smooth bedrock (Tile 0).
      * Physical Bouncing Drops: Spawns 2 to 4 physical bouncing ore items with
        3D parabolic trajectories.
