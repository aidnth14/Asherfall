ASHERFALL - ENEMY ECOSYSTEM & AI DIRECTOR SPECIFICATION (PERCEPTION 2.0)
================================================================================

1. ADAPTIVE AI DIRECTOR ARCHITECTURE (client/enemy/enemy.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Asherfall enemy system is coordinated by an adaptive AI Director (EnemyManager)
inspired by Left 4 Dead and Alien: Isolation.

A. Pacing Phases:
  - PHASE_BUILDUP: Low-threat exploration phase. Builds suspense while the player
    navigates terrain. Spawns are paced slowly (interval ~2.2s).
  - PHASE_PEAK: High-intensity engagement phase triggered when enemies make combat
    contact or when intensity reaches a critical threshold. Spawns coordinated
    flanking encounters (interval ~1.2s).
  - PHASE_RELIEF: 8-15 second respite window following combat clearance to allow
    healing, resource pickup, and repositioning.

B. Rolling 60-Second Performance Score & Dynamic Difficulty:
  - Tracks player combat performance over a rolling 60s sliding window:
      * Enemy Kill: +2.0 points
      * Damage Taken: -0.05 points per HP lost
      * Near-Death State (HP < 20%): -4.0 points
  - Cruising State (Score > +5.0):
      * PEAK phase spawns 25% faster.
      * Pack capacity increases by +1 (up to max 6 active enemies).
  - Struggling State (Score < -5.0):
      * PEAK phase spawns 40% slower.
      * Pack capacity reduced by -2 (minimum 2 enemies).
      * RELIEF phase stretches to 15.0 seconds for player recovery.

C. Dynamic Group Positioning (Anti-Stacking):
  - When 2 or more enemies target the player, they claim distinct angular slots
    distributed evenly around the player (e.g. 360° / count * index).
  - Prevents monsters from stacking into a single sprite clump.
  - Flankers curve around the player's flanks while vanguard units engage frontally.

D. Dynamic Trajectory Spawning & Emergence:
  - Spawns monsters 5 to 7 tiles ahead in the player's forward travel vector.
  - Directional ground dust, rock chips, or mist burst 0.8s prior to emergence.


2. UNIVERSAL UPGRADES & PERCEPTION 2.0 (client/enemy/base.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Perception 2.0 (Conical Vision & Noise Pings):
  - Sight Cone: Vision is no longer an omniscient radius; enemies possess a directional
    sight cone (default 100° field of view):
      * Standard (Rat, Bloodmonster, Demon): 100° FOV cone.
      * Aerial Sonar (Bat): 220° ultra-wide radar cone.
      * Ethereal Gaze (Skull): 160° broad spectral cone.
      * Seismic Tremor (Golem): 100° visual cone + 360° ground tremor sense (up to 4.5 tiles).
  - Noise Ping System: Loud actions emit acoustic shockwaves that alert enemies through fog/walls:
      * Gunfire: 12.0 tiles radius
      * Mining / Chopping Hit: 5.0 tiles radius
      * Melee Swing / Spin: 3.0 tiles radius
      * Sprinting / Dashing: 2.0 tiles radius
  - Spotting Beat (Alert Pause):
      * Upon initial visual detection, enemies enter STATE_ALERT for a brief hesitation:
        Default: 0.40s ("Wait, what was that?")
        Fast Reflexes (Bat, Skull): 0.20s
        Slow Tremor Sensing (Golem): 0.70s
  - Investigate State:
      * Losing line of sight does not reset immediately to IDLE.
      * Enemy enters STATE_INVESTIGATE, paths to the player's last known location,
        searches for 3.0 to 5.0 seconds, and only returns to IDLE if no trace is found.

B. Expanded 10-State Finite State Machine:
  1. STATE_IDLE: Ambient resting, scanning, or territory wandering.
  2. STATE_ALERT: Spotting beat pause with visual alert mark ("!").
  3. STATE_INVESTIGATE: Searching last known coordinates of a lost target or noise ping.
  4. STATE_CHASE: Coordinated pursuit utilizing angular slot assignments.
  5. STATE_WINDUP: Telegraph anticipation frame before hit lands; weapon/claw glints.
  6. STATE_ATTACK: Locked attack animation sequence; deals damage on strike frame.
  7. STATE_RECOVER: Post-attack recovery window where enemy cannot attack or turn fast.
  8. STATE_STAGGERED: Poise broken (hit 0 poise); 1.4s stun window with orbiting stars.
  9. STATE_RETREAT: Tactical repositioning or fleeing (low HP / cowardly state).
  10. STATE_DEATH: Disintegration and corpse decay sequence with item drops.

C. Poise & Stagger System:
  - Every enemy possesses an independent Poise Meter alongside HP.
  - Heavy weapons (M24 sniper, hammer, 360° sword spin, shotgun) rapidly drain poise.
  - Light weapons (pistols, SMGs) chip poise slowly.
  - Reaching 0 Poise triggers STATE_STAGGERED:
      * 1.4s complete stun window.
      * Golden orbiting stars rendered above the enemy's head.
      * Takes +25% bonus damage from all attacks while staggered.
  - Rapid Poise Regeneration: If an enemy takes no damage for 1.5 - 2.0 seconds,
    its poise meter rapidly regenerates back to 100% capacity.

5. INTELLIGENT PATHFINDING, DEATH ANIMATION & SWIMMING DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Intelligent Pathfinding & Route Planning (client/enemy/navigation.py):
  - Local Bounded 8-Directional A* Search:
      * Replaces naive vector pursuit with bounded grid exploration avoiding obstacles,
        solid boulders, spires, terrain cliffs, and deep water.
      * Fast execution (~0.04ms per search) with caching. Re-plans only when waypoints
        are depleted or the target drifts beyond tolerance (>1.5 tiles).
  - String-Pulling Path Smoothing (Funnel Algorithm):
      * Cleans jagged grid staircases into smooth, direct diagonal routes using line-of-sight
        raycasting across unobstructed tiles.
  - Tangential Obstacle Sliding:
      * When contacting a solid rock or prop corner, enemies dynamically decompose movement
        into sliding axes, preventing sticking or halting dead.
  - Terrain & Water Traversal Profiles:
      * Aerial Units (Bat, Skull): can_fly = True. Glides unimpeded across solid boulders
        and open water.
      * Amphibious Units (Rat): can_swim = True. Traverses rivers and water bodies freely.
      * Land Vanguard (Demon, Bloodmonster, Golem): can_fly = False, can_swim = False.
        Intelligently routes around solid obstacles and deep water.

B. Death with Animation & Ground Sinking:
  - Extended Death Sequence Duration:
      * Enemies smoothly play their full animated sprite sequence (Death animation) across
        1.15 seconds (death_total_dur = 1.15s).
  - Ground Sinking & Alpha Dissolution:
      * Vertical ground sink sinks corpse smoothly into the soil (progression * 6.0 * scale).
      * Smooth alpha fade dissolves corpse from 255 to 0 opacity during the second half
        of the death timer (prog > 0.45).
  - Species-Specific Visceral Death Particle Bursts:
      * Skull: Cyan ectoplasmic spark burst and soul dust.
      * Demon: Fiery magma embers, crimson smoke, and soot particles.
      * Blood Monster: Splattering crimson blood mist droplets.
      * Golem: Heavy stone rubble chips, gravel, and dust shards.
      * Bat: Shadowy violet wing flutters and dark mist.
      * Rat: Earthy brown fur tufts and pink flecks.

C. Swimming Animation & Waterline Dynamics:
  - Dynamic Freshwater Detection (Iso.is_water):
      * Identifies river channels, ponds, and rapids (tile IDs 104 to 114).
  - Concentric Expanding Water Ripples:
      * Two expanding elliptical ripple rings pulse outwards at the waterline.
  - Waterline Wave Bobbing:
      * Vertical harmonic buoyancy bobbing (sine wave 5.0 Hz) synchronizes with water motion.
  - Foam Spray & Waterline Cutoff:
      * Submerges the lower portion of the sprite with a foam waterline ring and spray.
