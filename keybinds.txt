ASHERFALL — CONTROLS
=======================

MENU & NAVIGATION
-----------------
Left Click            Select buttons, text fields, or settings
Escape                Go back, open/close pause menu
Enter                 Confirm selections

IN-GAME (ONLINE / SOLO)
-----------------------
W A S D               Move (or use Arrow Keys)
L-Shift               Dash (Cooldown)
Space                 Jump (In-world)
1 - 5                 Select Hotbar Slot (1-5) & Auto-Equip Weapon/Tool
Mouse Wheel           Camera Zoom In / Out
Shift + Mouse Wheel   Cycle Arsenal (Weapons and Tools)
Left Click            Attack / Shoot / Mine
Right Click           Mine / Chop / Break / Use Consumable from Active Slot
E                     Open / Close Adventurer's Backpack Inventory
F                     Interact / Open Chests & Loot Breakables / Use Item
C                     Open / Close Survival Crafting Bench
T or Enter            Open text chat (type message, press Enter to send, Esc to cancel)
V (Hold)              Push-to-Talk Voice Chat
Tab                   Cycle world rendering styles / palettes
R                     Regenerate world layout
- / =                 Zoom Out / Zoom In

ADVENTURER'S BACKPACK INVENTORY CONTROLS
-----------------------------------------
E or Esc              Close Backpack Inventory
Left Click            Pick up / Place / Swap Item Stack
Right Click           Pick up half stack / Place 1 item into slot
Shift + Left Click    Quick-Move Stack between Backpack & Hotbar
Shift + Right Click   Direct Use / Consume / Equip targeted item
F or Space            Use / Consume / Equip hovered slot item
Q                     Drop 1 hovered / cursor item to world
Shift + Q             Drop entire stack of hovered / cursor item to world
1 - 5 (Hovering)      Swap hovered slot directly with Hotbar Slot 1-5
Click Outside Window  Drop held cursor item into world

IN-GAME (LOCAL CO-OP)
---------------------
Player 1: W A S D     (Move)   | Space  (Jump)
Player 2: Arrows      (Move)   | RShift (Jump)
Player 3: I J K L     (Move)   | O      (Jump)
Player 4: T F G H     (Move)   | Y      (Jump)

DEBUG & ENGINE
--------------
F1                    Toggle Debug Info (If implemented)
F2                    Force regenerate soundtrack (Assets)

MENU & UI CONTROLLER NAVIGATION:
----------------------------------
- Virtual Mouse Cursor:  Left Stick (in menus) or Right Stick (anywhere) glides the mouse cursor smoothly.
- Mouse Click (Select):  A Button / Cross (✕) / RT (Right Trigger) clicks UI buttons, inputs & steppers.
- Drag Sliders:          Hold A Button or RT while moving the stick to drag volume & smoothness sliders.
- Snappy Focus (D-Pad):  D-Pad Up / Down snaps cursor and focus through all menu, setup, settings & pause items.
- Steppers & Sliders:    D-Pad Left / Right decrements / increments player count, audio volume, cam smooth, etc.
- Back / Cancel:         B Button / Circle (◯) / LT backs out of setup, closes settings, journals & pauses.
- Toggle Pause:          Start / Options / + (Plus)


LOCAL CO-OP CONTROLLER ASSIGNMENT:
----------------------------------
- Player 1: Gamepad 1 (or Keyboard W A S D)
- Player 2: Gamepad 2 (or Keyboard Arrows)
- Additional players seamlessly use plugged-in gamepads or configured keyboards.
- Gamepads can be connected or disconnected at any time (hot-plugging supported).
